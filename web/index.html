Revenge42

Client-side 4x4x4 solver. Three-phase reduction with search over many reductions, then Kociemba two-phase for the resulting 3x3x3. Nothing leaves the browser.

Outer turns U R F D L B and wide turns Uw Rw Fw Dw Lw Bw, each plain, 2 or '. Leave empty to solve a random state.
Measured over 150 scrambles solved at both budgets, the average was 42.527 moves at 5 s and 42.167 at 30 s. Longer searches do find shorter solutions, though the return is well under a move.
